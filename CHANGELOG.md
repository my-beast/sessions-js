# Changelog

## 0.1.2 - 2026-11-12

A press on a checkbox, a radio or a select is named by its label - never by its value ("on", "yes", an id) or a
select's options - and a press on a label is told once, not again for the click the browser hands on to the label's
control. Each upload carries the browser's clock (`sent`), so Sessions shows a recording's times and its length
right when a device's clock is off.
